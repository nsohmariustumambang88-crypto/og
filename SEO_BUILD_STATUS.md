## 💡 KEY DECISIONS MADE

1. **E-Commerce Focus** (not local delivery aggregator)
   - Target: National/international customers
   - Strategy: High-intent keywords + educational content

2. **Content Cluster Model** (blog → products → checkout)
   - Each blog article links to 2-3 product categories
   - Product pages link back to educational articles
   - Creates content flywheel

3. **Strain Pages as SEO Targets**
   - 30-50 strain pages = 30-50 long-tail keywords
   - Low competition (KD 7-15)
   - High search volume (8,000-50,000 monthly)
   - Quick wins for organic visibility

4. **Geographic Expansion Strategy**
   - Start: US + Europe (CBD products)
   - Follow: Canada, Australia (THC products)
   - Content per region supports local SEO

5. **Beginner-Friendly Content**
   - Every article assumes reader is new
   - Explains why (not just what)
   - Links to next steps in customer journey
