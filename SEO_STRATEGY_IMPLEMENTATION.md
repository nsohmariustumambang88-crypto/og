## PHASE 3: PRODUCT PAGE OPTIMIZATION

### Product Title Structure (SEO + Sales)

**Current (Likely):** "Product Name"
**Optimized:** "[Strain Name] [Product Type] | [Benefit] | [THC/CBD Info]"

Examples:
- ❌ "Apple Fritter"
- ✅ "Apple Fritter Indica Flower - Sleep & Relaxation (18% THC)"

- ❌ "CBD Oil"
- ✅ "Full-Spectrum CBD Oil 1000mg - Anxiety & Pain Relief"

- ❌ "Gummies"
- ✅ "CBD Gummies for Pain - 25mg Broad-Spectrum (Lab Tested)"

### Product Description Template

```
[150-250 words minimum]

1. Opening: What is it & what it does
   "Apple Fritter is an indica-dominant hybrid known for..."

2. Cannabinoid Profile
   "THC: 18% | CBD: <1% | Terpenes: Myrcene, Caryophyllene"
   Link to: → "Understanding Cannabinoids" article

3. Effects & Use Case
   "Best for: Sleep, relaxation, evening use"
   Link to: → "Best Indica Strains for Sleep"

4. Terpene Profile
   "Primary terpenes: Myrcene (sedating), Caryophyllene (pain relief)"
   Link to: → "Terpenes Guide"

5. How to Use
   "[Dosing recommendations based on article]"
   Link to: → Relevant dosing guide

6. Lab Testing
   "[Include COA link]"

7. Related Products
   "Similar strains: Blue Dream, Granddaddy Purple"
   "Try our Strain Quiz to find your perfect match"
   Links to: → Related products → Strain Quiz
```

### Product Category Pages

Each main category should have:
- **Intro paragraph** with category keyword (e.g., "Shop Premium Indica Flower...")
- **Filter options** (effects, THC range, terpene profile)
- **Educational section** linking to relevant blog posts
- **Best-seller highlights**
- **Related guides** - "New to Indica? Read our Sleep Guide"
